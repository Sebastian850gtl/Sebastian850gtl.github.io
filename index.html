Sebastian Baudelet-Aponte

Postdoctoral researcher at Inria Saclay, working with Jakob Ruess.

I study stochastic interacting particle systems and population models motivated by biology. My work focuses on the effect of microscopic mechanisms such as coalescence, growth and division on large scale population behavior.

Background

My PhD at Université Côte d’Azur, supervised by Thierry Goudon and Rémi Catellier, tackled multiscale modelling of branching in mycelial networks. I defended my thesis on 7 November 2025.
